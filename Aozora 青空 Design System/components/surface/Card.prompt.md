### Card

The default glass container. Three blur tiers, four tints. Sits over color — over plain white it loses its glass identity, so place it on a `--gradient-*` or photo bg.

```jsx
<Card tier="regular">A card on a sky-blue gradient.</Card>
<Card tier="thick" tint="dark" padding="lg">Modal-grade dark glass.</Card>
<Card tint="sun" padding="sm" radius="2xl">Promo callout.</Card>
```
