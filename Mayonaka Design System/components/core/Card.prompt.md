A panel for grouping content. `default` for normal sections, `raised` for hierarchy, `neon`/`cyan` for emphasis (track now playing, alerts, hero stats).

```jsx
<Card variant="neon" title="NOW PLAYING" meta="03:42 / 04:30">
  夜の高速 — Mariya Takeuchi
</Card>
```

Square corners only. Title is auto-uppercased display type. Avoid stacking many neon cards — one or two per view max.
