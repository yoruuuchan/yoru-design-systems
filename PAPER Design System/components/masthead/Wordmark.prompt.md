Sets a publication or personal wordmark in the display serif — use it anywhere a logo would go; PAPER has no drawn mark.

```jsx
<Wordmark text="PAPER" size="xl" tagline="editorial × analog design system" />
```

Sizes sm/md/lg/xl map to 15/19/30/46px. Tracking is fixed at 0.42em — do not override it; the optical letterspacing is the mark. For a person's site use their name: `<Wordmark text="Mira Andersson" size="sm" />`.
