A proof sheet — used to show process, selection and editing rather than finished work.

```jsx
<ContactSheet columns={4} stock="KODAK 400TX" note="Favourites" sequence="3/8"
  frames={[{src:"/1.jpg"},{src:"/2.jpg",mark:"circle"},{src:"/3.jpg",mark:"cross"},{src:"/4.jpg"}]} />
```

Marks are meaningful: circle a keeper, cross a reject, and mark no more than a quarter of the sheet. Sits on the dark plate — do not put it inside `data-mode="analog"` warmth.
