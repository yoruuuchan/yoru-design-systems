A gallery wall label. Pair it with a Plate in a two-column spread for exhibition and print-sales pages.

```jsx
<ExhibitionLabel artist="Mira Andersson" title="Drift" year="2023"
  medium="Archival pigment print" dimensions="50 × 70 cm" edition="Edition of 10"
  venue="Gallery North, Copenhagen" dates="Jan 12 – Feb 18, 2024" monogram="M/A" />
```

Museum conventions apply: artist, title, year, medium, dimensions, edition — in that order, nothing added.
