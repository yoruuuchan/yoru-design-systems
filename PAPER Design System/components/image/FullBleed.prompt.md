Editorial-mode section break: a band that runs the full width of the window, usually holding one photograph or one quote.

```jsx
<FullBleed dark>
  <PullQuote size="lg">You cannot photograph the return of the light.</PullQuote>
</FullBleed>
```

Two per long article at most. Its job is to give the eye a rest, so keep the content inside it to one idea.
