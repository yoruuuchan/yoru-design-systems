A captioned photograph — the workhorse of every essay and gallery page.

```jsx
<Figure number="Fig. 01" ratio="3/2" src="/coast.jpg"
  caption="Låtefossen at first light, 06:12. The exposure ran long by a full stop."
  credit="M. Sørensen" />
```

Captions are sentences with facts in them — time, place, stock, what happened. Never restate the headline. `align="side"` for wide plates in a single-column essay.
