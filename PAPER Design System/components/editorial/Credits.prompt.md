Closes a piece or a colophon. Everyone who touched the work, by role.

```jsx
<Credits items={[
  {role:"Photography", name:"M. Sørensen"},
  {role:"Words", name:"Ana Rehn"},
  {role:"Printing", name:"Narayana Press, Odder"}
]} />
```

Roles are nouns ("Photography"), not job titles ("Photographer"). Include the printer and the stock when the work was printed — that is the tone of this system.
