Byline strip: who made it, when, how long it takes to read.

```jsx
<MetaBlock items={[
  {label:"Words", value:"Ana Rehn"},
  {label:"Photographs", value:"M. Sørensen"},
  {label:"Published", value:"March 2026"},
  {label:"Reading", value:"12 min"}
]} />
```

Labels are single words. Four items is the usual maximum — beyond that use Credits.
