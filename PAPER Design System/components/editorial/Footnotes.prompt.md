Numbered notes. Reference them in the body with `<sup>1</sup>`, which is already styled in mono rust.

```jsx
<Footnotes title="Notes" items={[
  "Sunrise, Bodø, 21 Dec: 11:47. Sunset: 13:12.",
  "All plates printed at 24 × 30 cm on Ilford MGFB."
]} />
```

Footnotes hold facts the body cannot carry — measurements, dates, sources. Not asides or jokes.
