Opens an article, an essay or an interview.

```jsx
<ArticleHeader kicker="Photo Essay" title="The Slow Return of" emphasis="Winter Light"
  standfirst="Four months on the Baltic coast, photographed on expired film." />
```

Titles are noun phrases, not questions or promises. Keep them under eight words so the display size can stay large. `emphasis` italicises the tail — use it once, on a title's last two or three words.
