The essay body. Paragraphs after the first are indented, not spaced — that is the print convention this system keeps.

```jsx
<TextBlock dropCap columns={2}>
  <p>The road north empties in November…</p>
  <p>We kept a notebook of the returns…</p>
</TextBlock>
```

One drop cap per article, on the opening paragraph only. For Chinese pass `lang="zh"` — leading opens to 1.9 and the drop cap is dropped.
