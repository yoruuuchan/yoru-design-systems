A single print mounted on card, with the caption written into the bottom margin.

```jsx
<MemoryCard width={280} caption="Sunday light in the kitchen" date="APR 12 2026" rotate={-1}>
  <img src="/photo.jpg" alt="" />
</MemoryCard>
```

Square by default. Rotate at most one card in a group, by no more than 1.5°.
