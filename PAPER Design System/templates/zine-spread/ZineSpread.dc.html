Small Observations

The way the curtain moves before the window opens. A cup left on the table. Footsteps down the hall. These are the notes I keep returning to.1

None of it is worth photographing on its own. Together, over a winter, it is the only record I have of that year.

单独看都不值得拍。可是一个冬天攒下来，那就是我留下的全部记录。

1.Kodak Portra 400, available light throughout.
