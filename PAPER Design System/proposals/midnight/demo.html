The Coast Later

Four months on the Baltic coast, photographed on expired film and printed the following spring.

WordsA. Lindqvist
PhotographsA. Lindqvist
PublishedApr 2026
StockKodak 400TX

The bus stopped running in October. After that the road belonged to the weather, and to whoever had reason to be out in it. Most mornings there was no reason, and the road did what the fields did: nothing, patiently, in grey.

What changed was the light. Not more of it — there was never more of it — but the angle. By November it arrived already leaving, raking across the water at an hour when the shops were still open, and the whole town stood in its own shadow looking west.

"The light arrived already leaving, and the town stood in its own shadow looking west."

We printed in March, when the snow on the darkroom roof melted and dripped through the vent. The prints came out darker than the days had been. We kept them that way.

光线回来的那几周，我们几乎不说话，只记录时间和温度。印出来的照片比那些日子本身更暗，我们就让它们保持那样。

Notes

¹ Expired 2019. Shot at box speed regardless; the fog metered for us.

² Developed in HC-110 (B), 6 min, 20°C. Two rolls were pushed a stop by mistake and are better for it.
