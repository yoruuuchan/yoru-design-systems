The Slow Return of Winter Light

Four months on the Baltic coast, photographed on expired film and printed the following spring.

WordsAna Rehn
PhotographsM. Sørensen
PublishedMarch 2026
Reading12 minutes

The road north empties in November. What remains is a particular quality of light that arrives late, stays low, and leaves early — an hour, then two, then a whole afternoon of it by February.

We kept a notebook of the returns: sunrise times, film stock, the temperature at the moment of exposure. The record is unremarkable on its own and becomes something else entirely when read in sequence.

光线回来的那几周，我们几乎不说话，只记录时间和温度。

You cannot photograph the return of the light. You can only photograph the things it lands on.

M. Sørensen — interview, 2025
